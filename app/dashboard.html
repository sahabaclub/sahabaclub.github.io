    How to sign in and what you can use

    Signing in the first time

    1. Go to microsoft365.com and choose Sign in.
    2. Enter your @sahabaclub.com address above — not your personal email.
    3. Enter the temporary password, then set a password of your own when Microsoft asks. It will not let you continue without doing this.
    4. You may be asked to add a phone number or the Microsoft Authenticator app. This is for recovering your own account — the club cannot see it.

    What comes with it

    • Email at your @sahabaclub.com address, in Outlook on the web or the Outlook app.
    • Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote in the browser, saving straight to the cloud.
    • 1 TB of OneDrive storage.
    • Microsoft Teams for club events and for talking to other members.
    • Forms, Planner, Lists and Sway, which is what the hackathons run on.

    Worth knowing

    • On your phone, install Outlook and Teams and sign in with the same address.
    • Signing in on the web needs nothing installed — useful on a borrowed machine.
    • The desktop Office apps are not included on this licence. The browser versions do the same work.
    • This is a real mailbox on a real tenant. Treat it as you would a work account: it is how the club will reach you.
