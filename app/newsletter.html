Goes only to members who've opted in. Check the audience before you send — there's no unsend.

Sending is not available yet. This composer and the audience count work, but there is currently no way to send a newsletter to members: the sender was removed because it could not record who it had already mailed, and an interrupted run could only be fixed by mailing everyone a second time.

To reach members, nothing here can do it yet. To reach contacts — event attendees and prospects — use Campaigns, which sends in reviewable batches, tracks every recipient, and carries a working unsubscribe link.

⚠ Note that those are two different lists. A member who turned the newsletter off in Settings is not excluded by Campaigns, because Campaigns reads the contacts list and never looks at member preferences.
