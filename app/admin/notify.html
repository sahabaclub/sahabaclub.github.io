What this does, and what it doesn’t

It writes a notification into each member’s dashboard and adds one to the count on their menu. It does not send an email, and it does not send anything to a phone — push notifications aren’t switched on yet. Email is reserved for the things with a deadline attached: Microsoft 365 expiry, membership renewal, and the hour before an event you registered for.

There is no unsend. Once the confirm is accepted the rows exist in people’s dashboards and nothing here can take them back.

Members who have switched this kind off in their settings will not receive it, and this page cannot tell you who they are — those preferences are readable only by the member they belong to. That is why the number before sending says “up to” and the number afterwards does not.
