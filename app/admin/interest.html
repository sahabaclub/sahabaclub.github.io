What “notified” means here

A registration is recorded first and the club is emailed second. The timestamp in that last column is written only once the mail has actually been accepted for delivery — so a row marked not emailed is a real registration whose notification never went out, not a half-finished one. The person on it registered successfully and is waiting to hear from somebody.

Details are unverified. Nobody signing this form was asked to prove the email address or the phone number is theirs, and they were not asked to create an account — that is the point of a public interest form. Treat it as a lead, not a record.
