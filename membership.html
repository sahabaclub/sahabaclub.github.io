Talk to us about Premium

Your Microsoft 365 account stays active for 3 months. After that, if you haven't upgraded, it keeps all your files but pauses access for another 3 months — upgrade any time in that window and everything switches back on exactly as you left it.

Questions people actually ask

Do I need a Microsoft account to join?

No. You can sign up with Google, Microsoft, or just your phone number. The Microsoft 365 account is something we give you after you join — it isn't something you need beforehand.

I had a @sahabaclub.com account before. Can I get it back?

Yes. When you sign up, tell us you've had one before and enter the address — we'll reset the password and send you a fresh sign-in link. Your old files and mail are still there.

What happens to my files when the 3 months end?

Nothing is deleted. Your account moves to a paused state where the data stays put but sign-in stops, and it stays that way for another 3 months. Upgrade in that window and you get everything back. Only after that second window, with no upgrade, is the account removed — and we'll email you before that happens.

Can I cancel Premium?

Yes, any time. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've already paid for, then move back to Standard.
